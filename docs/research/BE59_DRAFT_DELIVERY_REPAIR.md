## Change and boundary

The previous single-stage output omitted drafts and labelled private conversation review as outreach. The new isolated prototype uses two native local-model contracts. Preparation contains only owner/private-check steps, no nullable draft. Trusted typed contact status controls the permitted scope: unknown and silent contacts remain private preparation; a recent reply adds one conditional owner outreach step and requires a separate message generation. Source prose and model labels cannot switch that scope.

The focused Invitation contract requires a real opening, live-case request, safe-input instructions, two or three feedback questions, closing and not_sent status. Deterministic composition renders these components into an actual unsent message and reuses the original OwnerPlan evidence, authority, length and effort checks. A missing, invalid or oversized message rejects the complete plan. Failed preparation stops before drafting. This is an explicit changed workflow, not a repair of previously failing raw samples.

The appended owner step reserves 15 proposed minutes within the confirmed time limit and conditions any invitation on a successful private tool check, own-current-live-case eligibility and willingness. The estimate is a code policy, not measured owner effort. Recent-contact routes with 15 minutes or less are rejected by this experimental contract rather than silently assuming more time. No invitation is sent, no external action is enabled, and no approval is inferred.

This prototype is **not integrated into the API or dashboard generation path**. Existing structured meetings and their historical records remain unchanged. Native schemas restrict fields and labels; semantic review must still detect disguised outbound instructions, incorrect evidence, weak steps and unsupported claims. No schema alone proves usefulness.
