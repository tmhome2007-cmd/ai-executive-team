## What worked

Shared contracts connected skills, source snapshots, approvals, artifacts and checkpoints before expanding them. Revocation and withdrawal now block continuation without rewriting earlier evidence. The final audit checked those features together rather than only in isolation.

The owner-requested layout change addressed a concrete complaint about crowding. Grouped navigation and separate business tabs received direct positive feedback. The office remained a bounded prototype, preserving accessible alternatives and the €0 budget rather than implying a finished simulation.

`./company start` and `./company audit` provide short entry points that preserve the existing key and database. This addresses earlier owner feedback about confusing branch changes, multiple terminals and test credentials; we have not measured time saved.

## What failed or needs improvement

The dashboard redesign left several browser checks using old headings and navigation assumptions. Combined audit attempts then exposed stale selectors, hidden elements, fractional datetime handling and a local-midnight publication-date boundary. Earlier failed reports were retained and a complete successful rerun followed the fixes. These were integration and fixture problems we should catch earlier, not owner mistakes.

The run inspector advertised continuation after a skill or workflow was withdrawn, although the authoritative claim rejected it. Its summary now reflects current eligibility. Error responses also lacked the intended privacy headers; the middleware now supplies them. Cross-feature negative cases need to be part of each story’s acceptance, not deferred until sprint end.

AI output quality remains a material gap: six benchmark attempts failed and narrow checks cannot assess usefulness. Long reports can hide mistakes from an owner who reads diagonally. We need representative useful outputs, brief next steps and explicit missing facts before adding more controls.

The dependent PR stack grew large. Separate stories aided traceability, but postponed merges increase the amount the owner must review together. We should seek acceptance and an authorized merge at coherent checkpoints. No hours, velocity, revenue or customer demand are inferred from this sprint.
