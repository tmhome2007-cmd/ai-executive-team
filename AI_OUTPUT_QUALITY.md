# Shared AI output quality

Meetings, preparation, reviewed planning work, discussions and autonomous follow-up
use the same factual discipline. Persona influences tone and professional priorities;
it cannot supply missing facts, owner approval or successful outcomes.

The shared text checks detect empty/oversized output, unfinished placeholders,
selected invented-consent phrases, selected unsupported causal/component-health
inferences and selected contradictions with labelled source details. Existing
quantitative, incident, historical-memory and repeated-analysis checks remain.

For meeting tasks without tools, an invalid draft receives one direct text-only
correction attempt with its original requirements, current evidence, persona and
specific issues. No tools are supplied to that correction. The resulting text and
its quality policy are bound to the durable checkpoint; completed retries reuse it.
Tasks with tool authority are never replayed or corrected by another model call.
Unresolved text carries an explicit unverified-draft warning. A warning is not
approval, a truth certificate, or a replacement for independent review.

Discussion corrections include phase format and actual published-claim quotation
checks. Critiques that still fail attribution are withheld. Existing stop-condition
and revision safeguards remain. Warnings from earlier rounds remain visible in the
parent decision instead of disappearing when the final round passes format checks.

Structured executive plans retain strict JSON/task/dependency/budget validation.
Shared checks also reject task text containing unresolved quality findings. Estimates
remain proposals, not measured costs or spending authority.

Daily research captures bounded excerpts from distinct successful tool reads.
Every required URL must be present and have an exact source quotation in the
summary. Repeated reads of one page cannot satisfy source coverage. Failed source
coverage, quotations or shared summary checks prevent an evidence-backed meeting.
Downstream meetings receive actual excerpts as evidence, separate from CSO prose.
These are membership checks, not proof that an interpretation follows from a quote.
Older cached research without captured records fails closed for manual review.

Prompts and quality-policy inputs changed. An interrupted older run may fail its
input-bound checkpoint check; inspect it rather than automatically replaying tools.
Completed historical decisions and conversations are preserved.

This improves controllable publication behavior; it does not certify every possible
model answer, guarantee useful critique generation or guarantee financial returns.
The local 8B model can still misinterpret valid quotations or miss subtleties.
Owner approval, budget, scope and execution gates remain separate.
